Hi, I'm Noah Gibson.

Husband, father, pastor, and Fort Wayne local — passionate about people, the local church, and the everyday moments where faith becomes real. Glad you're here.

About Me

More than a title.

I'm a pastor — but that's not the most interesting thing about me. I'm a husband and a dad first, and everything I do flows from that.

I believe the church is most powerful when it's honest, when it makes room for doubt, and when it looks more like a family than a program. That conviction has shaped every role I've served in.

I grew up with deep roots in Fort Wayne, Indiana. Outside of ministry, you'll find me in the workshop doing woodworking, out on a trail hiking with my family, or tinkering with the latest tech. I'm someone who loves building things — whether that's furniture, community, or a team worth being part of.
