About Me

Passionate about people & purpose

Noah Gibson is a communicator and Associate Pastor based in Fort Wayne, IN, driven by a profound passion to help others Find and Follow Jesus.

He wholeheartedly believes in the significance of the Church conducting its work with Excellence, Diligence, and a fervent heart for God. Noah aspires to empower the church to cultivate leaders and shepherd their congregation with the mission of reaching the lost, the hurting, and those neglected in life.

In his personal life, Noah finds joy in his marriage to his dedicated ministry partner, Caroline, and together they are raising two daughters, Finley and Holland.
